### 🔌 wol-micro

Smart home & microcontroller projects by [@Lindenson](https://github.com/Lindenson) — firmware that actually runs in the house.

| Project | What it is | Hardware |
|---|---|---|
| [**garageAlarms**](https://github.com/wol-micro/garageAlarms) | Smoke & motion alarms delivered to Telegram, with a power-cut-safe delivery queue — in service | ESP32-S3 |
| [**garageAlarms2.0**](https://github.com/wol-micro/garageAlarms2.0) | Hubless Zigbee alarm network for the garage and adjacent rooms — Telegram, Pushover, MQTT | ESP32-S3 / H2 |
| [**LuzRele**](https://github.com/wol-micro/LuzRele) | Light relay with delay timer, PIR motion & light sensors, OLED menu and buzzer melodies | STM32F411 |
